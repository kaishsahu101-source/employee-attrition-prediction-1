ℹ️ About This System

This machine learning system predicts whether an employee is likely to leave the company based on various factors including job satisfaction, work-life balance, compensation, and career growth opportunities.

🎯 Key Features

  • ✅ Predict employee attrition risk (Yes/No)
  • ✅ Identify key factors influencing attrition
  • ✅ Provide probability scores for risk assessment
  • ✅ Help HR teams take preventive measures

📊 Top Factors Affecting Attrition

  1. Overtime - High impact on retention
  2. Job Satisfaction - Critical factor
  3. Years at Company - Experience matters
  4. Work-Life Balance - Employee wellbeing
  5. Monthly Income - Compensation fairness
